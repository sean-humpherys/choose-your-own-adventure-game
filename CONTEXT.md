# Choose Your Own Adventure

A single-player, text-driven fantasy adventure played at the console in English, Spanish, or French. The player creates a character, picks a path through the mountains, and lives or dies by dice.

## Language

### The adventure

**Adventure**:
One playthrough, from character creation to an ending. A player gets one Adventure per run of the game.
_Avoid_: Session, run, story

**Path**:
A direction the player chooses at the fork: north, south, or exit. Choosing a Path is the player's only navigational decision.
_Avoid_: Route, branch, direction

**Encounter**:
A scripted meeting on a Path that the player interacts with, such as the Dragon or the Merchant. An Encounter offers choices and may change the character.
_Avoid_: Event, scene, meeting

### The character

**Character**:
The persona the player creates and plays: a Name, a Race, an Occupation, three rolled statistics, and the equipment they carry.
_Avoid_: Avatar, hero, PC

**Race**:
The ancestry chosen at creation. It modifies which statistics get bonuses when rolled.
_Avoid_: Species, ancestry, kind

**Occupation**:
The character's profession, chosen at creation. It determines the Weapon they start with.
_Avoid_: Class, job, role

**Health Points**:
How much Damage a combatant can absorb before being defeated. Reaching zero ends the Adventure in defeat.
_Avoid_: HP as a prose word, hit points, life, vitality

**Maximum Health Points**:
The Health Points a Character is created with, fixed for the Adventure. Healing can restore Health Points up to this ceiling and never past it. A Character has one; the Dragon does not.
_Avoid_: Max HP, full health, starting health

**Strength**:
The statistic an Attack Roll is measured against. Higher Strength means landing more blows.
_Avoid_: Power, might

**Agility**:
The statistic a Defense Roll is measured against. Higher Agility means dodging more blows. Agility describes evasion only, never armor.
_Avoid_: Speed, dexterity, nimbleness

### Combat

**Attack Roll**:
The roll that decides whether a blow lands, measured against the attacker's Strength.
_Avoid_: To-hit, hit check

**Defense Roll**:
The roll that decides whether the target dodges a landed blow, measured against the target's Agility.
_Avoid_: Dodge check, evasion roll, save

**Damage**:
The Health Points a successful, undodged blow removes. Rolled against the attacking Weapon's maximum.
_Avoid_: Hurt, injury

**Damage Reduction**:
A flat amount subtracted from incoming Damage before it reaches Health Points. Armor is the only source. Damage never falls below zero.
_Avoid_: Protection, defense, armor class, mitigation, absorption

### Equipment and trade

**Equipment**:
Everything a Character carries to use: one Weapon, at most one Armor, and at most one Healing Potion. Gold is not Equipment. It is what Equipment is bought with.
_Avoid_: Inventory, items, gear, belongings, pack

**Weapon**:
The single armament a character attacks with. A character carries exactly one; acquiring another replaces it.
_Avoid_: Arm, gear, item

**Armor**:
Worn equipment that grants Damage Reduction. A character carries at most one.
_Avoid_: Shield, protection, plate

**Magical**:
The quality of the wares bought from the Merchant, distinguishing them from the starting equipment an Occupation grants. Magical equipment is strictly better than its starting counterpart.
_Avoid_: Enchanted, legendary, upgraded

**Healing Potion**:
A Magical ware bought from the Merchant and drunk during combat to restore Health Points, never above Maximum Health Points. A Character carries at most one, and drinking it consumes it.
_Avoid_: Elixir, remedy, cure, heal as a noun

**Merchant**:
The Encounter on the south Path who sells one piece of Magical ware: Armor, a Weapon, or a Healing Potion. A player meets the Merchant once per Adventure.
_Avoid_: Trader, vendor, shopkeeper, shop

**Gold**:
The currency a character carries, fixed at creation, and the only thing the Merchant accepts. A purse affords exactly one Magical item.
_Avoid_: Coins, money, currency, credits
