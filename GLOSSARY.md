# Choose Your Own Adventure

A text adventure where a player creates a character, picks a path, and faces encounters such as the dragon and the merchant.

## Language

**Merchant**:
A trader met once per game on the South path who sells one item: Magical Armor or a Magical Weapon.
_Avoid_: Shop, vendor, trader

**Magical Armor**:
Armor that absorbs a fixed amount of damage from every hit that gets past the player's defense.
_Avoid_: Shield, protection, defense

**Magical Weapon**:
The player's occupation weapon after enchantment, with a higher maximum damage. It is the same weapon, not a replacement.
_Avoid_: New weapon, upgrade

**Gold**:
The player's currency: a fixed amount every character starts with, spent only at the Merchant.
_Avoid_: Money, coins, currency
